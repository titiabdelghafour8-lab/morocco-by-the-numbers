Method

  • Data quality first. A month counts only if no more than three days are missing; a hydrological year counts only if all twelve months do. {{years_complete}} of {{years_total}} years pass. The latest file stops partway through the year, so the monitor ends in {{latest_month}}.
  • Drought index. SPI with a gamma distribution, from the climate-indices reference implementation, fitted on every year of this station's record, at 3, 6 and 12 months.
  • Trend. Mann-Kendall test with Sen's slope on yearly totals, plus four versions that correct for persistence; Pettitt test for a single change point.
  • Rain and river. Runoff ratio and a straight-line fit of yearly mean flow on yearly rain, both fitted on 1971–1999 and applied to 2000 onwards.

Limits

  • One basin, one main rain gauge. This says nothing about drought in the rest of Morocco.
  • The index compares each period with this station's own history; it is not a national or official drought declaration.
  • Daily records reach the portal about a year late.
  • The rain-and-river comparison describes a gap; it does not prove what caused it.
