A forecast is shown only if a model beats the benchmark of repeating last year's monthly changes. Forecasts are uncertain: the average miss is a guide to how far off they can be.

Method

  • Rates are computed from HCP's monthly index: year on year, month on month, and a three-month annualised rate on the seasonally adjusted index (robust STL decomposition).
  • Contributions are weight × price change ÷ last year's headline index, so they add up to the headline rate. The weights come from a non-negative least-squares fit of the headline index on its twelve groups.
  • Movers use a robust z-score of each product's monthly change against all its earlier months.
  • City groups come from hierarchical clustering on the correlation of seasonally adjusted monthly changes.
  • Forecasts: three models on the log index (repeat last year's monthly changes; exponential smoothing with a damped trend and seasonality; a seasonal ARIMA), tested from rolling origins three months ahead.

Limits

  • The index covers the cities HCP surveys; rural prices are not included.
  • HCP revises past months, so earlier figures here can differ from those announced at the time.
  • Weights are estimates, not HCP's official figures.
  • A model that beat the benchmark in the past can still miss, especially when prices turn quickly.
